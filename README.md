## Methodology:​
Implement, use and showcase different AI techniques in demonstrations that show their pro, cons and different use cases through the context and vessels of animals.​

### Pathfinding:​

* Predator animals use biological equivalent of pathfinding to target prey, making a good AI technique to use for emulating their behaviour​.​

### Finite State Machine:​

* Many animals such as scavengers act in ways like states to stay safe from predators, like searching, waiting and running.​​

### Sensors:​

* All animals (including us humans) uses senses to take in enviromental inputs (sight, smell, hearing, taste ) as extra info about their surrounds, AI Sensors can roughly replicate some these like sight smell and hearing.​​
